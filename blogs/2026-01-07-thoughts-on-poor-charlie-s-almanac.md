I recently finished reading Poor Charlie’s Almanac , a collection of speeches and writings from Charlie Munger , Warren Buffett’s long-time business partner at Berkshire Hathaway. Munger passed away in November 2023 at the age of 99, just weeks before his 100th birthday. The book consists of many of Munger’s speeches over the years, most of which were delivered at graduation ceremonies or to alumni groups.
