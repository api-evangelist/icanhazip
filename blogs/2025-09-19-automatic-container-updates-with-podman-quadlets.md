Running containers at home or in production often means juggling updates across multiple services. While orchestration platforms like Kubernetes handle this automatically, what about those simple deployments on a single host? Podman’s quadlet system integrates containers directly with systemd, and when combined with automatic updates, you get a robust solution that keeps your containers fresh without manual intervention.
