I moved over to Hyprland as my primary desktop environment several months ago after wrestling with some other Wayland desktop environments. It does plenty of things well and finally allowed me to do screen sharing during meetings without much hassle. A couple of small utilities, hyperidle and hyprlock , handle idle time and locking the screen when I step away from my desk.
